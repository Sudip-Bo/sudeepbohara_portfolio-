# Content Guide & Editorial Standards

This document establishes the copywriting standards, structural length, writing style, tone, and section-by-section examples for the Sudip Bohara Digital Agency portfolio.

---

## 1. General Copywriting Philosophy
* **Tone**: Confident, articulate, direct, authoritative, and restraint-driven. Avoid tech jargon, fluff, or generic buzzwords ("cutting-edge", "game-changing").
* **Writing Style**: Short active sentences, bulleted takeaways, and high scannability.
* **Grammar & Voice**: First-person singular (`I`) for direct accountability, switching gracefully to agency collective (`We / SB Studio`) for client engagements.

---

## 2. Section-by-Section Content Guidelines

### A. Hero Section
* **Tone**: Bold, authoritative, high-craft.
* **Length**: Title (max 6-8 words), Sub-headline (20-30 words max).
* **Example**:
  * *Headline*: "Engineering High-Impact Digital Experiences for Growth."
  * *Sub-headline*: "I partner with forward-thinking companies to design and build high-converting websites, web apps, and design systems with extreme precision."

### B. About Section
* **Tone**: Grounded, professional, story-driven.
* **Focus**: Engineering values, problem-solving mindset, client outcomes over personal credentials.
* **Length**: 2-3 short paragraphs (under 120 words total).

### C. Services Section
* **Tone**: Technical clarity, outcome-oriented.
* **Structure**: Monospaced index, short title, 2-line summary, 4 key deliverable pills.
* **Example**:
  * *Title*: "Next.js Web Applications"
  * *Summary*: "Custom web apps built for sub-second page loads, SEO dominance, and effortless content administration."

### D. Portfolio / Case Studies
* **Tone**: Empirical, metric-supported, structural.
* **Structure**: Client name, Industry, Core challenge, Solution delivered, Verifiable outcome tag (`Performance Focused`, `Mobile First`).
* **Empty State Copy**: `"New case studies coming soon."`

### E. Process Section
* **Tone**: Methodical, predictable, high-trust.
* **4-Step Cadence**: `01 Discovery & Strategy` → `02 Architecture & UI Design` → `03 Next.js Engineering` → `04 Launch & Scale`.

### F. Contact Section
* **Tone**: Welcoming, responsive, clear commitment.
* **Elements**: Location badge (`📍 Nepal`), reach (`Working with clients worldwide`), SLA (`Usually replies within 24 hours`), Privacy Policy checkbox text: `"I agree to the Privacy Policy"`.
* **Empty State Inbox Copy**: `"No enquiries yet."`

### G. Footer
* **Tone**: Minimal, clean, navigational.
* **Copyright**: `© 2026 Sudip Bohara. All rights reserved.`
